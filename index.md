## How to use this list {#how-to-use}

- Each park is a National Park Service site in the Passport to Your National Parks® American Revolution / America 250 set — get its passport cancellation stamp at the park's visitor center or bookstore desk.
- 📍 Every park name links to its Google Maps location.
- ☐ Tick a park off as you collect its stamp. Counts: **Northern 16 · Central 19 · Southern 11 · Total 46**.
- The 3 DC entries (Constitution Gardens, Thomas Jefferson Memorial, Washington Monument) are also on the Nov 19–22, 2026 DC Stamp Hunt route.

## Notes on the pins {#notes}

- **Trails:** Washington-Rochambeau (listed under Pennsylvania) and Overmountain Victory are multi-state trails — each is pinned at one anchor point (Yorktown, VA and Gaffney, SC), not along the whole route.
- **Cumberland Gap** spans KY / TN / VA — the pin is the visitor center near Middlesboro, KY.
- **Boston NHP** is a dispersed park — the pin is the Charlestown Navy Yard / Bunker Hill end.
- **Fire Island** is pinned at the Wilderness Visitor Center (Smith Point / Mastic Beach end).
- **Charles Pinckney NHS** is listed under Charleston but sits in Mount Pleasant, SC; **Kings Mountain NMP** is pinned at the park near Blacksburg, SC.
- **Prince William Forest Park's** town was cut off in the source list — it is at Triangle, VA.
- Source: *American Revolution Parks Checklist* (Passport to Your National Parks® – Northern, Central & Southern Regions), list supplied Oct 23, 2026 — 46 parks total.
